# Equipment capacity

Checked 2026-10-04 following the owner's request for product counts in the equipment comparison.

The local Compact Cooler proposal specifies adjustable wire shelves but no numeric stock capacity. The Smart Fridge proposal specifies five adjustable shelf levels and suitable product categories, but no numeric count. These proposal documents remain the primary source.

The [manufacturer's current equipment page](https://boostinc.com/pos/) does not give unit capacity for the Compact or Classic coolers. Its published figures of 480 items apply to the separate digital vending range and must not be assigned to our proposed coolers.

A [Boost smart-fridge article](https://boostinc.com/vending-to-smart-fridge-conversion-3500-sales-in-90-days/) mentions up to 80 SKUs alongside a 24-inch screen. That describes product varieties rather than the total number of physical items, and the article does not establish capacity for our 13.3-inch Compact configuration or the unspecified Smart Fridge Series model in our proposal.

The current editable table row therefore reads "Product capacity — Depends on packaging and shelf layout", with a Latvian equivalent. It appears in homepage comparisons and equipment specification pages in both languages.

To replace this with numbers, obtain the exact manufacturer model/configuration and a validated unit count for a stated product mix or packaging size. Record SKU variety separately if useful. Do not infer stock capacity from the photos or convert cabinet litres into an item count without package measurements and shelf-layout validation.
