# Baltic Vending Solutions

B2B WordPress website for customers considering vending equipment purchase, rental and custom machine wrapping. Latvian is the default language; English pages are paired through Polylang. The proposed equipment is Boost inc Compact Cooler and Smart Fridge. Native WordPress pages, menus, widgets and blocks remain editable. Private quote enquiries are the main conversion.

The site serves businesses with their own packaged food/drinks and entrepreneurs evaluating a first sales point. Explain product suitability, equipment configuration, responsibilities and a route to a quote. Prices, legal company details, territory and service inclusions remain subject to owner confirmation. Do not invent profit guarantees or assume restocking and food supply are included.

The wrapping editor is an Operate page within the existing BVS identity. Visitors use the supplied Smart Fridge model to try colours, artwork, a logo and drawing on three visual wrap panels, then download a mockup or attach it to an enquiry. It is not a print-production template. One design draft saves automatically in this browser so visitors can resume later. Contact details are excluded; Start again or clearing site data removes it. Attachments are private. Existing cookie choices and Local no-email/noindex safeguards remain in effect.

[CLIENT_PROFILE.md](CLIENT_PROFILE.md) provides the established client truth and audience assumptions. [STYLE_GUIDE.md](STYLE_GUIDE.md) remains the existing detailed visual authority. [docs/design-editor.md](docs/design-editor.md) defines the editor and private-file handling.
